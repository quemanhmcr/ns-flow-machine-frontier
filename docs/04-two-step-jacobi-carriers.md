# 04 — Two-Step Continuation, Jacobi, and Carrier Geometry

For a nested path

\[
(J,K)\to M,
\qquad
(I,M)\to L,
\]

the full Euler coefficient is

\[
\boxed{
(\lambda_K-\lambda_J)(\lambda_M-\lambda_I)
 f_{MJK}f_{LIM}.
}
\]

The factor \(\lambda_M-\lambda_I\) survives the velocity/Biot–Savart reuse. Degenerate equal-curl continuation can vanish exactly.

At bracket level define the weighted compositions \(A,B,C\). Lie Jacobi gives

\[
\boxed{A+B+C=0,}
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{|A|^2\le2(|B|^2+|C|^2),
\qquad
\max(|B|,|C|)\ge|A|/2.}
\]

But the full Euler signed-curl gaps can erase the cyclic full companions. This repository treats the following as a hard guard:

\[
\boxed{
\text{weighted Jacobi controls bracket composition, not a full-Euler heat bill}.
}
\]

For Fourier carriers

\[
m_1=j+k,
\quad m_2=k+i,
\quad m_3=i+j,
\quad \ell=i+j+k,
\]

closure gives

\[
\boxed{m_1+m_2+m_3=2\ell,}
\qquad
\boxed{\max_r|m_r|\ge 2|\ell|/3.}
\]

Hence high output forces a comparable intermediate **location**, but not yet nonzero work there.

At the low-frequency endpoint, a helicity-flip critical boost satisfies the sharp Galilean suppression

\[
2\sqrt{|k||\ell|}\,|g(q,\ell,-k)|
\le\frac{3\sqrt6}{16}|q|.
\]

At every nonzero carrier the viscous endpoint is

\[
\nu|m|^2.
\]
