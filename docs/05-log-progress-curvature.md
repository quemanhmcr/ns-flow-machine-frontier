# 05 — Near-Lossless Steps Must Make Vanishing Logarithmic Progress

For a strict physical heterochiral UV triad, normalize the forward child to radius \(M\), let \(D,S\in(0,1)\) with \(D+S>1\), and let \(R>0\) be the physical current.

Define the logarithmic forward progress

\[
\boxed{
F_{\log}
=(D+S)R\log\frac1{\max(D,S)}
}
\]

and the occurrence-wise Nijenhuis/quadratic-variation weight

\[
\boxed{
Q_\triangle
=M^2(1-D)(1+S)(D+S)R.
}
\]

Then over the entire strict physical triangle,

\[
\boxed{M^2F_{\log}\le Q_\triangle.}
\]

The constant \(1\) is sharp at a degenerate boundary where transfer retention tends to one while logarithmic displacement tends to zero.

Therefore the universal law is continuous:

\[
\boxed{
\text{arbitrarily efficient retention}
\Longrightarrow
\text{arbitrarily small scale progress}.
}
\]

The historical obstruction is that \(Q_\triangle\ge0\) is tied to one oriented triad occurrence. A sum of \(Q_\triangle^+\) over a chosen decomposition is not intrinsic. The full Nijenhuis curvature is representation-free but signed and may cancel coherently.
