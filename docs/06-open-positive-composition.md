# 06 — Open Frontier: Representation-Free Positive Composition

The exact critical-uphill tangent is

\[
s=(\Lambda-K/E)u,
\]

with

\[
\|s\|_2^2=\frac{EZ-K^2}{E}.
\]

The exact normalized escape action is

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{escape}}
=
\frac{\kappa^2}{N^2(EZ-K^2)}
=
\left|
\left\langle
\widehat\omega,
\widehat s\times u
\right\rangle
\right|^2.
}
\]

Critical mean-curl escape obeys

\[
K/E\to\infty
\Longrightarrow
\int_0^T\mathcal A_{\mathrm{escape}}\,dt=\infty.
\]

The remaining theorem is to prove

\[
\boxed{
\int_0^T\mathcal A_{\mathrm{escape}}(u(t))\,dt<\infty
}
\]

on every finite smooth positive-energy interval.

## What must compose

A successful proof has to combine, without introducing an analyst-made gross traffic budget:

- actual signed-curl continuation gaps;
- bracket-level Jacobi compatibility;
- comparable Fourier carrier geometry;
- Galilean suppression of nearly uniform carriers;
- diagonal heat at rate \(\nu|m|^2\);
- the sharp one-occurrence inequality \(M^2F_{\log}\le Q_\triangle\).

The target mechanism is

\[
\boxed{
\text{persistent nontrivial logarithmic continuation}
\Longrightarrow
\text{intrinsic non-cancelling heat-visible cost}.
}
\]

## What is not allowed

The proof must not rely on:

1. one-step sign monotonicity;
2. a global \(10/13\) loss law;
3. a full-Euler companion lower bound inferred directly from Jacobi;
4. \(\sum Q_\triangle^+\) over a selected triad decomposition;
5. a scalar radial ledger that forgets orientation/phase.

The remaining loophole is coherent many-interaction cancellation and recycling while maintaining net logarithmic escape.
