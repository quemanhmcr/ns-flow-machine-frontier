# 03 — One-Step Freedom and Constrained Euler Acceleration

Set

\[
F=F_E=J_uCu,
\qquad
\Lambda=|C|,
\qquad
\kappa=\langle\Lambda u,F\rangle,
\qquad
F'=DF_E(u)[F].
\]

The Euler invariants imply

\[
\boxed{\langle u,F'\rangle=-\|F\|_2^2,}
\qquad
\boxed{\langle Cu,F'\rangle=-\langle CF,F\rangle.}
\]

Thus the two normal components of Euler acceleration are fixed by the present current.

Where \(u\) and \(Cu\) are independent, write

\[
P_{\operatorname{span}\{u,Cu\}}\Lambda u=a\,u+b\,Cu,
\qquad
 g_K=P_T\Lambda u.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\kappa'_E
=\langle F,(\Lambda-a-bC)F\rangle
+\langle g_K,P_TF'\rangle.
}
\]

The remaining freedom is exactly **tangent turning**.

A fixed physical Fourier triangle with fixed helicities realizes all four sign quadrants of

\[
(\kappa,\kappa'_E).
\]

Therefore there is no primitive one-step sign-decay theorem. Any true self-frustration must be historical/compositional.
